# Earthquake

> Drop · Cover · Hold on

## Before

> Secure heavy items · Know where to shelter · Pack a bag

### Warning signs

- Earthquakes happen suddenly with no warning, so prepare ahead of time
- The Thai Meteorological Department reports an earthquake near you. Be ready for aftershocks
- If you are by the sea and the ground shakes hard, or the sea pulls back fast, a tsunami may come
- People report an earthquake near you in the app

### Get ready

1. **Fix tall cabinets and shelves to the wall** So they cannot fall on you
2. **Keep heavy things on low shelves** Never above where you sleep
3. **Find a safe spot in each room** Under a strong table, away from glass and cabinets
4. **Know where the gas valve and main switch are** So you can turn them off fast
5. **Pack an emergency bag** Drinking water, dry food, your medicine, a flashlight, a whistle, a power bank
6. **Agree on a meeting point with your family** An open area away from buildings and power poles

## During

> Drop · Cover · Hold on

### Do now

1. **Drop to the ground** Before the shaking knocks you down
2. **Get under a strong table** If there is none, cover your head and neck with your arms
3. **Hold on to the table leg** Until the shaking stops
4. **Stay where you are until the shaking stops** Then leave the building

### Don't

- Don't run out of the building while the ground is shaking. Things can fall on you
- Don't use the elevator
- Don't stand near windows, glass or tall cabinets
- Don't light fires, matches or candles. Gas may be leaking

### If you are in bed

1. **Stay in bed** Cover your head with a pillow
2. **Lie face down** And wait until the shaking stops

### If you are outside

1. **Move to an open area** Away from buildings, signs, trees and power lines
2. **Drop and cover your head** Until the shaking stops

### If you are driving

1. **Stop in an open area** Away from bridges, expressways and tall buildings
2. **Stay in the car** Until the shaking stops

### If you are by the sea

1. **Go to high ground now** If the ground shakes hard or the sea pulls back. Do not wait for a warning
2. **Move far from the coast** Follow the tsunami evacuation signs
3. **Stay on high ground until it is declared safe** More than one wave may come

## After

> Expect aftershocks · Smell for gas · Use the stairs

### In this order

1. **Check yourself and people around you for injuries** Call 1669 if someone is badly hurt
2. **Put on shoes** To protect your feet from broken glass
3. **Leave the building by the stairs** And go to an open area
4. **Turn off the gas if you smell it** Open the windows and leave the house
5. **Check your home for cracks** If walls, floors or pillars are cracked, do not go back in yet
6. **Take photos of the damage** Use them as evidence to claim aid from the district or local office
7. **Follow news from the authorities** Do not believe or share rumors

### Watch out for

- **Aftershocks** The ground can shake again. If it does, drop, cover and hold on
- **Cracked buildings** They can collapse later
- **Fallen power lines or poles** Stay at least 10 meters away
- **Gas smell** Do not flip light switches or light a fire

### If you are trapped under rubble

1. **Cover your mouth and nose** With cloth or your shirt, to keep out dust
2. **Tap on a pipe or wall** So rescuers can hear you, or blow a whistle
3. **Shout only now and then** Shouting all the time tires you and makes you breathe in dust
4. **Move as little as you can** So you do not stir up dust

## Emergency numbers

- **1784** Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation, report a disaster or ask for help
- **1669** Medical emergency
- **191** Police
- **199** Fire
