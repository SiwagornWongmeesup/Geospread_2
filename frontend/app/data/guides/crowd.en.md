# Crowd crush

> Arms up to protect your chest · Stay on your feet · Move out sideways

## Before

> Find the exits · Avoid barriers · Agree on a meeting point

### Warning signs

- People are packed so tight that bodies touch on all sides and you cannot move on your own
- You are pushed along by waves of people without taking steps yourself
- It is hard to breathe because you are being squeezed
- You hear people shouting or screaming up ahead
- People report a dense crowd near you in the app

### Get ready

1. **Find the exits and fire exits when you arrive** At least 2
2. **Agree on a meeting point with your group** If you get separated, meet there
3. **Arrive early or leave late** Avoid the busiest times
4. **Wear closed-heel shoes** And nothing that can catch or tighten around your neck
5. **Charge your phone fully**
6. **Carry children or hold their hand at all times** And write your phone number on them

## During

> Arms up to protect your chest · Stay on your feet · Move out sideways

### Do now

1. **Raise your arms in front of your chest** Like a boxer's guard, to keep room to breathe
2. **Stand firm** Bend your knees a little, feet apart
3. **Go with the flow, don't fight it** Then move out sideways a little at a time
4. **Save your energy** Do not shout or struggle
5. **Get people around you to tell those behind to stop pushing** If you can say it together

### Don't

- Don't stand against walls, fences or poles. You will be crushed
- Don't bend down to pick up something you dropped
- Don't push the people in front of you
- Don't walk against the crowd

### If you fall

1. **Get up right away if you can**
2. **If you cannot, lie on your side** Curl up and protect your head with your arms
3. **Look for a chance to stand** When the pressure eases

### If someone falls in front of you

1. **Shout to the people behind that someone has fallen**
2. **Help pull them up** If you can do it without falling yourself

## After

> Go to the meeting point · Check for injuries · Call 1669

### In this order

1. **Go to the meeting point** Check that everyone in your group is there
2. **Check yourself for injuries** See a doctor if your chest hurts or breathing is hard
3. **Call 1669** If someone is unconscious and not breathing, do CPR
4. **Tell officials if someone is missing** Describe them and where they were last seen

### Watch out for

- **Another crush** As people leave. Wait a while
- **Chest injuries** Some people only feel them later

## Emergency numbers

- **1669** Medical emergency
- **191** Police
- **199** Fire
- **1784** Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation, report a disaster or ask for help
