# Billboard collapse

> Get clear · Protect your head · Call 1669

## Before

> Avoid signs in wind · Spot leaning signs · Report it

### Warning signs

- A billboard or steel frame sways or leans, has loose bolts, or is badly rusted
- You hear steel creaking or the sign banging in strong wind
- The sign panel is torn, loose or flapping
- Scaffolding or a temporary sign does not look stable
- People report a billboard collapse near you in the app

### Get ready

1. **Report it to the district or municipal office** If a sign or steel frame looks unsafe
2. **Check storm and strong wind warnings** From the Thai Meteorological Department
3. **In strong wind, pick a route that does not pass under signs** Or scaffolding

## During

> Get clear · Protect your head · Call 1669

### Do now

1. **Move away from the sign now** If it sways, leans, or you hear steel creaking
2. **Go farther away than the sign is tall** A sign can fall as far as its height
3. **Protect your head with your arms or a bag** As you move away
4. **Shelter inside a strong building** If the wind is still strong
5. **Call 1669 or 199** If the sign falls on people or cars. Say exactly where

### Don't

- Don't shelter from rain or wind under billboards or steel frames
- Don't park under or near signs in strong wind
- Don't lift or pull the steel frame yourself. Wait for rescuers
- Don't go near if power lines are lying on it. You could be electrocuted
- Don't crowd around to watch

### If you are driving

1. **Don't stop under signs or scaffolding** Keep going and park in an open area away from signs and trees
2. **If a sign blocks the road** Don't drive over or under it. Turn around and find another way

### If someone is trapped under the sign

1. **Call 1669 and 199 right away**
2. **Check for power lines first** If there are any, don't go near
3. **Talk to the trapped person** Let them know help is coming
4. **Don't try to lift the steel frame yourself** The sign may shift and press down harder

## After

> Stay away · Avoid power lines · Tell the officials

### In this order

1. **Stay away from the fallen sign** What is left of the frame may fall too
2. **Tell the district or municipal office** So they remove it and check other signs nearby
3. **Keep the way clear for ambulances and rescue trucks**
4. **Take photos of the damage** Use them as evidence for aid and insurance claims

### Watch out for

- **Fallen power lines or poles** Stay at least 10 meters away. Call the Metropolitan Electricity Authority 1130 or the Provincial Electricity Authority 1129
- **Sign panels and steel still hanging** They can fall later
- **Sharp steel edges** Be careful not to get cut

## Emergency numbers

- **1669** Medical emergency
- **199** Fire
- **1130** Metropolitan Electricity Authority (Bangkok, Nonthaburi, Samut Prakan)
- **1129** Provincial Electricity Authority
- **1784** Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation, report a disaster or ask for help
