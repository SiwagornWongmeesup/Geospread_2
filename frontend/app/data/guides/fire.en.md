# Fire

> Get out now · Stay low · Call 199

## Before

> Know your way out · Keep an extinguisher · Check your plugs

### Warning signs

- You smell burning, or see smoke from a plug, wire or electrical device
- The building fire alarm goes off
- People report a fire near you in the app

### Get ready

1. **Know at least 2 ways out** At home and at work
2. **Keep fire exits clear** And never lock fire doors
3. **Keep a fire extinguisher at home** And know how to use it
4. **Install a smoke alarm** And check that it works
5. **Don't plug power strips into each other** Unplug devices you are not using
6. **Agree on a meeting point outside** So you can check everyone got out

## During

> Get out now · Stay low · Call 199

### Do now

1. **Shout "Fire!" to warn others** Press the fire alarm if there is one
2. **Leave the building now** Do not pack anything
3. **Stay low and crawl out** If there is smoke. The air near the floor is easier to breathe
4. **Cover your mouth and nose with cloth**
5. **Call 199** Once you are outside. Say where it is and if anyone is trapped

### Don't

- Don't use the elevator
- Don't go back inside to get things
- Don't open a door if the handle is hot
- Don't put water on an oil or pan fire
- Don't fight a large fire yourself. Get out first

### If you have to go through a door

1. **Touch the door and handle with the back of your hand** Before opening
2. **If it is hot, don't open it** Find another way
3. **Close doors behind you** It slows fire and smoke

### If you are trapped in a room

1. **Close the door and block the gap underneath** With a wet cloth
2. **Call 199** Say which floor and room you are in
3. **Signal from the window** Wave a bright cloth or use a flashlight
4. **Don't jump from a height**

### If your clothes catch fire

1. **Stop** Do not run
2. **Drop to the ground** Cover your face with your hands
3. **Roll** Until the fire is out

## After

> Cool burns · Don't go back in · Wait for officials

### In this order

1. **Go to the meeting point and count people** If someone is missing, tell the officials. Do not go back in to look
2. **Wait until officials say it is safe** Before going back in
3. **Take photos of the damage** Use them as evidence for aid and insurance claims
4. **See a doctor if you breathed in a lot of smoke** Even if you feel fine

### Watch out for

- **A fire that looks out** It can start again
- **The building structure** A burned building may collapse
- **Smoke** It can sting your eyes and make you cough or short of breath. See a doctor if it does

### If you get burned

1. **Run clean water over the burn** For about 20 minutes
2. **Take off rings and watches** Before swelling starts
3. **Cover with a clean cloth** Do not put toothpaste, fish sauce or balm on it. Do not pop blisters
4. **Call 1669** If the burn is large or deep, or on the face, hands or genitals

## Emergency numbers

- **199** Fire
- **1669** Medical emergency
- **191** Police
- **1784** Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation, report a disaster or ask for help
