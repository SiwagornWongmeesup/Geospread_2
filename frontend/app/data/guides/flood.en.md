# Flood / flash flood

> Cut power · Get high · Stay out of water

## Before

> Lift items · Charge up · Know your way out

### Warning signs

- Heavy rain for several hours, or heavy rain upstream
- Water in canals or streams rising fast and turning muddy brown
- Branches and debris floating down in unusual amounts
- A loud roaring sound coming from upstream (a sign of a flash flood)
- People nearby reporting flooding in the app

### Get ready

1. **Move things up high** Valuables, documents, appliances, and power strips
2. **Charge everything fully** Your phone and power banks
3. **Pack 3 days of essentials** Drinking water, dry food, your medicines, a flashlight, and a whistle
4. **Put key documents in a waterproof bag** ID card, house registration, land title, and bank books
5. **Move your car to high ground before the water comes** Once the water rises, you may not be able to drive out
6. **Plan your way out** Find the nearest safe point or high ground and how to get there
7. **Pick a meeting point with your family** If you can't reach each other, meet there

## During

> Cut power · Get high · Call 1784

### Do now

1. **Switch off the main breaker** Only while you are still dry. If you are standing in water, don't touch it
2. **Turn off the gas** Close the valve on the tank all the way
3. **Go to the highest place** An upper floor, the roof, or a hill
4. **Send your location to someone close** Say where you are and who is with you
5. **Save your battery** Lower screen brightness and close apps you don't need

### Don't

- Don't walk through water near power poles, power lines, or transformers
- Don't walk through fast-moving water. Even ankle-deep water can knock you over
- Don't drive through moving water. About 30 cm is enough to sweep away a small car
- Don't touch plugs or switches while you are wet
- Don't go back inside for belongings while the water is rising

### If it's a flash flood

1. **Climb to high ground right away** Leave your things. A flash flood arrives within minutes
2. **Move sideways out of the water's path** Head to high ground. Don't run along the waterway
3. **Stay away from streams and narrow bridges** Until the water goes down

### If you are trapped at home

1. **Stay at the highest point** An upper floor or the roof
2. **Call 1784 or 1669** Give your location, how many people are with you, and whether anyone is hurt
3. **Pin a help request in the app** So people nearby and rescue teams can see you
4. **Signal so people can see you** Wave a bright cloth, use a flashlight, or blow a whistle

### If you are trapped in a car

1. **Unbuckle your seatbelt** As soon as water starts coming in
2. **Lower the windows** While the car's power still works
3. **Get out through the window** Climb onto the roof and wait for help

### If someone gets an electric shock

1. **Cut the power first** Don't touch the person with bare hands
2. **Push the wire away with something dry** Use something that doesn't conduct electricity, like dry wood
3. **Call 1669** If they are unconscious and not breathing, start CPR

## After

> Check power · Clean up · Watch for illness

### In this order

1. **Wait for the all-clear** Before you go back into your home
2. **Look for cracks** If walls, floors, or posts are cracked, don't go in yet
3. **Have an electrician check before using power** Plugs and wiring that were underwater must be checked every time
4. **Take photos of the damage** Use them as evidence to claim aid from the district or local office
5. **Throw away food that touched floodwater** Including fridge food if the power was off for a long time
6. **Clean with disinfectant** Open the windows to air out the house and prevent mold
7. **Drink boiled or bottled water** Until you are sure the tap water is safe

### Watch out for

- **Snakes and venomous animals** that fled the water into your home. Poke around with a stick before lifting things
- **Leptospirosis** If you waded through water and then get a high fever or calf pain, see a doctor right away and tell them you were in floodwater
- **Water-soaked feet** Wash your feet with soap and dry them well every time after wading
- **Mosquitoes** Standing water after a flood is where they lay eggs. Empty any containers holding water

### If a snake bites you

1. **Stay still** Keep the bite lower than your heart
2. **Take off rings and bracelets** Before the swelling starts
3. **Go to a hospital right away** Remember what the snake looked like. Don't cut, suck, or tie off the wound

## Emergency numbers

- **1784** DDPM, disaster reports and rescue
- **1669** Medical emergency
- **191** Police
- **199** Fire
