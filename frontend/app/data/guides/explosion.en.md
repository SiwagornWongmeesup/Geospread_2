# Explosion

> Get down · Move away · Call 199

## Before

> Check the gas · Turn off the valve · Know the exits

### Warning signs

- You smell leaking gas (a bad smell like rotten eggs)
- You hear hissing from a gas tank or gas pipe
- A gas tank is bulging or rusty, or the gas hose is cracked
- You see a suspicious object or an unattended bag in a crowded place
- People report an explosion or gas leak near you in the app

### Get ready

1. **Turn off the gas valve every time you finish cooking**
2. **Check the gas hose and regulator** Replace them if they are cracked or stiff
3. **Keep gas tanks in a well-ventilated place** Not in a closed room or under the stairs
4. **Know the exits of buildings you use** Home, work and malls you visit often

## During

> Get down · Move away · Call 199

### Do now

1. **Drop to the ground and cover your head** As soon as you hear an explosion
2. **Move away from the blast area as fast as you can** There may be another explosion
3. **Stay away from glass and windows** Broken glass can fly
4. **Cover your mouth and nose with cloth** If there is smoke or dust
5. **Call 199 once you are far away** Say where it is and if anyone is hurt

### Don't

- Don't go closer to look or film
- Don't use the elevator
- Don't go back inside to get things
- Don't touch or move a suspicious object

### If you smell gas

1. **Don't flip switches or unplug anything** A spark can cause an explosion
2. **Don't light a flame or lighter**
3. **Turn off the gas valve**
4. **Open doors and windows** To let the gas out
5. **Leave the house** Then call a technician or 199 from outside

### If you find a suspicious object

1. **Don't touch it** And don't use your phone right next to it
2. **Tell people nearby to move away**
3. **Get far away and call 191** Say where it is and what it looks like

## After

> Stay away · Help the injured · Wait for officials

### In this order

1. **Stay away from the blast site** Until officials say it is safe
2. **Tell rescuers if someone is missing** Say where they were last seen
3. **Keep the way clear for ambulances and fire trucks**
4. **See a doctor** Even if you are only slightly hurt. A blast can injure your ears and lungs without you noticing

### Watch out for

- **Another explosion** Especially where there are several gas tanks or chemicals
- **The building structure** A building hit by a blast may collapse
- **Broken glass and metal** Wear closed shoes if you must walk through

### If someone is bleeding heavily

1. **Press hard on the wound with a clean cloth** Keep pressing. Don't let go
2. **Call 1669**
3. **Don't pull out anything stuck in the wound**

## Emergency numbers

- **199** Fire
- **1669** Medical emergency
- **191** Police
- **1784** Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation, report a disaster or ask for help
