# Shooting / assault

> Run · Hide · Fight

## Before

> Know the exits · Trust your instincts · Tell someone close

### Warning signs

- You hear what sounds like gunshots, firecrackers or screaming
- You see people running from the same direction
- You see someone with a weapon, or acting aggressive or threatening
- You feel someone is following you
- People report violence near you in the app

### Get ready

1. **Always note the exits** In malls, cinemas and crowded places
2. **If you feel unsafe, leave** Don't wait until you are sure
3. **Walk in bright, busy places** At night
4. **Share your location with someone close** When traveling alone
5. **Save 191 and 1669** In your phone

## During

> Run · Hide · Fight

### Do now

1. **Run as far away as you can** Leave your things. Go away from the gunfire
2. **If you can't run, hide** In a room you can lock, and block the door with heavy things
3. **Silence your phone and turn off the lights** Stay quiet, away from doors and windows
4. **Call 191 when it is safe** Say where you are, what the attacker looks like, and the weapon
5. **Fight only as a last resort** When your life is in real danger. Use what is around you and act together with others

### Don't

- Don't go to look or film near the scene
- Don't hide in a place with no way out if you can still run
- Don't post where you are hiding or where the police are on social media
- Don't run toward police or hold anything in your hands when you meet them

### If you meet the police

1. **Raise your hands with your palms open**
2. **Do what the police tell you**
3. **Don't get in their way** They must reach the attacker first, then come back for the injured

### If you are attacked or robbed

1. **Give up your things** Your life matters more than your belongings
2. **Shout for help** And run to where there are people
3. **Remember the attacker** Face, clothes, vehicle, license plate and which way they went

### If someone is bleeding heavily

1. **Press hard on the wound with a clean cloth** Keep pressing. Don't let go
2. **Call 1669**
3. **Don't pull out anything stuck in the wound**

## After

> Report it · See a doctor · Take care of your mind

### In this order

1. **Stay somewhere safe** Until officials say it is over
2. **Tell people close to you that you are safe**
3. **See a doctor** Even if you are only slightly hurt
4. **Report it at the police station** Tell them what you saw
5. **Keep the evidence** Do not wash or throw away the clothes you wore when attacked
6. **Follow news from the authorities** Do not believe or share rumors

### Watch out for

- **Stress afterward** Trouble sleeping, being easily startled and nightmares are normal. If it does not get better, call the mental health hotline 1323
- **Violent photos and videos** Watching them again makes stress worse, and they should not be shared

## Emergency numbers

- **191** Police
- **1669** Medical emergency
- **1323** Mental health hotline
- **1300** Social assistance center (domestic violence)
