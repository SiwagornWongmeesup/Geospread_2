# Assault

> Get away · Find people · Call 191

## Before

> Trust your instincts · Walk in bright places · Tell someone close

### Warning signs

- Someone is acting aggressive or threatening, or is drunk and looking for trouble
- A fight between people keeps getting worse
- You feel someone is following you
- People report a fight or assault near you in the app

### Get ready

1. **If you feel unsafe, leave** Don't wait until you are sure
2. **Walk in bright, busy places** At night
3. **Share your location with someone close** When traveling alone
4. **Save 191 and 1669** In your phone

## During

> Get away · Find people · Call 191

### Do now

1. **Move away from the aggressive person** Don't argue or challenge them
2. **Go where there are many people** A shop, police box or convenience store
3. **Shout for help** So people nearby know
4. **Call 191** Say where you are and what the attacker looks like

### Don't

- Don't step in yourself if the attacker has a weapon. Call 191
- Don't respond in a way that makes things worse
- Don't chase the attacker

### If you are attacked or robbed

1. **Give up your things** Your life matters more than your belongings
2. **Shout for help** And run to where there are people
3. **Remember the attacker** Face, clothes, vehicle, license plate and which way they went

### If you see someone else being attacked

1. **Call 191** Before stepping in
2. **Make noise or call people nearby** So the attacker knows they are being watched
3. **Film only from a safe distance** As evidence for the police, not for social media

### If someone is bleeding heavily

1. **Press hard on the wound with a clean cloth** Keep pressing. Don't let go
2. **Call 1669**
3. **Don't pull out anything stuck in the wound**

## After

> Report it · See a doctor · Take care of your mind

### In this order

1. **Go somewhere safe** And tell someone close to you
2. **See a doctor** Even if you are only slightly hurt. Ask for a medical certificate as evidence
3. **Report it at the police station** Tell them what you saw
4. **Keep the evidence** Do not wash or throw away the clothes you wore when attacked

### Watch out for

- **Stress afterward** Trouble sleeping, being easily startled and nightmares are normal. If it does not get better, call the mental health hotline 1323
- **If the violence is at home** Get help from the social assistance center 1300

## Emergency numbers

- **191** Police
- **1669** Medical emergency
- **1300** Social assistance center (domestic violence)
- **1323** Mental health hotline
