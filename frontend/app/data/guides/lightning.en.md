# Lightning

> Get indoors · Avoid open ground · Avoid tall things

## Before

> Count to 30 · Know where to shelter · Get it right

### Warning signs

- You hear thunder or see lightning
- **The 30-second rule** When you see lightning, count the seconds until you hear thunder. If it is less than 30 seconds, lightning can reach you. Take shelter now
- The Thai Meteorological Department issues a thunderstorm warning
- People report lightning near you in the app

### Get ready

1. **Know where to shelter** The nearest strong building or car with a hard roof
2. **Postpone outdoor activities** If there is a thunderstorm warning
3. **Install a lightning rod** If your home is in an open or high area

### Get it right

- **Phones do not attract lightning** The danger is standing in an open area
- **A car is safe because of its metal roof and body around you** Not because of the tires. Convertibles and motorcycles are not safe
- **Lightning can strike the same place again** Especially tall things like poles, buildings and big trees
- **Lightning can strike before the rain starts** If you can hear thunder, you are close enough to be struck

## During

> Get indoors · Avoid open ground · Avoid tall things

### Do now

1. **Get inside a strong building** Or a car with a hard roof, with the windows closed
2. **Unplug electrical devices** That you do not need
3. **Stay away from open doors and windows**
4. **Stay inside until 30 minutes after the last thunder**

### Don't

- Don't shelter under big trees, power poles or steel structures
- Don't stay in open areas, rice fields, sports fields, beaches, or in the water
- Don't shower, wash dishes, or touch metal pipes
- Don't use devices that are plugged in
- Don't hold up umbrellas, golf clubs or other long metal objects

### If you are outside and cannot find shelter

1. **Always look for shelter first** Crouching lowers the risk only a little
2. **Crouch down with your feet together** Head down, cover your ears. Do not lie flat
3. **Spread out several meters apart** If you are in a group

### If you are driving

1. **Park away from trees and power poles**
2. **Turn on your hazard lights**
3. **Stay in the car** And do not touch metal parts

### If someone is struck by lightning

1. **Touch and help them right away** A person struck by lightning does not carry electricity
2. **Call 1669**
3. **Do CPR** If they are unconscious and not breathing

## After

> Wait 30 minutes · Check on the injured · Check your wiring

### In this order

1. **Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder** Before going outside
2. **Take anyone struck by lightning to a doctor** Even if they seem fine. It can affect the heart and nerves
3. **Check your electrical devices** If you smell burning or see smoke, cut the power and call 199

### Watch out for

- **Fires started by lightning** On roofs, trees or power lines
- **Fallen power lines or poles** Stay at least 10 meters away. Call the Metropolitan Electricity Authority 1130 or the Provincial Electricity Authority 1129

## Emergency numbers

- **1669** Medical emergency
- **199** Fire
- **1784** Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation, report a disaster or ask for help
- **1130** Metropolitan Electricity Authority (Bangkok, Nonthaburi, Samut Prakan)
- **1129** Provincial Electricity Authority
