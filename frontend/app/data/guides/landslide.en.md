# Landslide

> Watch for signs · Escape sideways · Go to high ground

## Before

> Know your risk · Watch for signs · Know your way out

### Warning signs

- Heavy rain for many hours or days in mountains or foothills
- Stream water turns muddy, or soil and trees flow down with the water
- Stream water rises or drops fast while it is still raining
- New cracks appear in the ground, roads or house walls
- Trees, power poles or fences start to lean
- You hear a loud rumbling or rocks knocking together from the mountain
- People report a landslide near you in the app

### Get ready

1. **Ask your local office if your home is in a risk area** Especially homes next to slopes or waterways
2. **Plan your way out** To high ground that is not in the path of the slide
3. **Pack an emergency bag** Drinking water, dry food, your medicine, a flashlight, a whistle, a power bank
4. **Listen for warnings during heavy rain** On the radio, village loudspeakers and from the village head
5. **Agree on a meeting point with your family** If you cannot reach each other, meet there

## During

> Escape sideways · Go to high ground · Call 1784

### Do now

1. **Leave your home now** Do not pack anything
2. **Escape sideways** Away from the path of the slide
3. **Go to stable high ground** Away from slopes and waterways
4. **Call 1784** Say where you are and how many people are with you

### Don't

- Don't run down the path the soil is sliding along
- Don't cross streams or bridges with fast water
- Don't go back inside to get things
- Don't shelter in a house at the foot of a slope

### If you cannot get away

1. **Curl into a tight ball**
2. **Protect your head with your arms**

### If you are driving

1. **Watch for soil or rocks falling on the road** Drive slowly
2. **Don't drive over soil piled on the road** Turn around and take another way

## After

> Stay away · Wait for the all-clear · Report missing people

### In this order

1. **Stay away from the slide area** It can slide again
2. **Tell rescuers if someone is missing** Say where they were last seen. Do not search on your own
3. **Wait until it is declared safe** Before going home
4. **Check your home for cracks** If walls, floors or pillars are cracked, do not go in yet
5. **Take photos of the damage** Use them as evidence to claim aid from the district or local office
6. **Drink boiled or bottled water** Until you are sure the water is clean

### Watch out for

- **Another slide** Especially if it is still raining
- **Flash floods** May follow. Stay away from streams
- **Fallen power lines or poles** Stay at least 10 meters away
- **Damaged roads and bridges** They may give way

## Emergency numbers

- **1784** Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation, report a disaster or ask for help
- **1669** Medical emergency
- **191** Police
- **199** Fire
