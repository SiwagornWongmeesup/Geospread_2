# Building / sign collapse

> Get clear · Protect your head · Call 1669

## Before

> Watch for cracks · Avoid signs in strong wind · Report it

### Warning signs

- New cracks in walls, pillars or beams, or old cracks getting wider
- Loud creaking or cracking sounds from the structure
- Doors or windows no longer close properly, or the floor tilts
- Dust or bits of plaster fall from the ceiling
- A billboard or steel frame sways or leans, has loose bolts, or is badly rusted
- People report a building or sign collapse near you in the app

### Get ready

1. **Report it to the district or municipal office** If a building or sign looks unsafe
2. **Know the exits of buildings you use** Home, work and malls you visit often
3. **Stay away from billboards and scaffolding** During heavy rain or strong wind

## During

> Get clear · Protect your head · Call 1669

### Do now

1. **Leave the building now** If you hear creaking or see cracks spreading
2. **Use the stairs** Never the elevator
3. **Protect your head with your arms or a bag** As you leave
4. **Go to an open area** Farther from the building than it is tall
5. **Call 1669 or 199** Say where you are and if anyone is trapped

### Don't

- Don't use the elevator
- Don't go back inside to get things
- Don't climb into the rubble to help. Wait for rescuers
- Don't light a fire or lighter. Gas may be leaking
- Don't crowd around to watch

### If you cannot get out in time

1. **Get under a strong table** Or against an inside wall, away from glass
2. **Protect your head and neck with your arms**

### If you are trapped under rubble

1. **Cover your mouth and nose** With cloth or your shirt, to keep out dust
2. **Tap on a pipe or wall** So rescuers can hear you, or blow a whistle
3. **Shout only now and then** Shouting all the time tires you and makes you breathe in dust
4. **Move as little as you can** So you do not stir up dust or shift the rubble
5. **Send your location to someone close** If your phone still works

## After

> Stay away · Report missing people · Wait for the all-clear

### In this order

1. **Stay away from the collapse site** It can collapse again
2. **Tell rescuers if someone is missing** Say where they were last seen
3. **Keep the way clear for ambulances and rescue trucks**
4. **Wait for an engineer to check the building** Before going back in
5. **Take photos of the damage** Use them as evidence for aid and insurance claims

### Watch out for

- **Parts still hanging** They can fall later
- **Broken power lines and gas leaks** Stay away and tell the officials
- **Concrete dust** Wear a mask if you must stay nearby

## Emergency numbers

- **1669** Medical emergency
- **199** Fire
- **191** Police
- **1784** Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation, report a disaster or ask for help
